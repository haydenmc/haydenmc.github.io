I discovered this recipe [online](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/270371/banana-cheesecake-with-banana-cream-pie-topping/)
and it quickly became one of my favorites. It combines two things I love:
bananas and creamcheese!

# Equipment

- 9-inch Springform Pan
- Mixing Bowls
- Whisk
- Electric Mixer (or a really strong arm)
- Parchment paper

# Ingredients

| **Cake**                      |                  |
|-------------------------------|------------------|
| Oreos                         | 1 package        |
| Unsalted butter               | ~2 tbsp          |
| Low-Fat/Fat-Free Cream Cheese | 3 × 8oz packages |
| White Sugar                   | 3/4 cups         |
| Eggs                          | 3                |
| Bananas                       | 2 - 3            |
| Vanilla Extract               | 1 tsp            |

| **Topping**                                   |                   |
|-----------------------------------------------|-------------------|
| Cold Milk                                     | 1 1/4 cups        |
| ***Instant*** Pudding Mix (Vanilla or Banana) | 1 × 3.4oz package |
| Fat-Free Frozen Whipped Topping, Thawed       | ~1/2 cup          |
| Bananas                                       | 1 - 2             |

# Recipe

## Cake

1. Prepare equipment:
   - Pre-heat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
   - Cut a circle of parchment paper to line the bottom of your springform pan.
   - Optionally cut a strip of parchment paper to run along the sides of the pan
     to make separation easier.
1. Prepare ingredients:
   - Mash Oreos in a ziplock bag or similar until you have 2 - 2.5 cups.
   - Mash 1 - 2 bananas until you have 1/2 - 1 cups.
   - Soften ~2 tbsp of butter so it can bind the mashed Oreos into a crust.
1. Make crust:
   - Mix cookie crumbs (~2 cups) and melted butter (~2 tbsp) together in a bowl.
   - Press mixture into the bottom of a 9-inch springform pan (ensure parchment
     is lining the bottom).
1. Make cake mix:
   - Mix cream cheese (3 × 8oz package) and sugar (3/4 cup) in a bowl using
     electric mixer until smooth and well-blended.
   - Add eggs one at a time, mixing after each addition.
   - Stir in mashed banana (~1/2 cup) and vanilla extract (1 tsp).
   - Pour mixture on top of the crust in the springform pan.
1. Bake in the preheated oven until center is almost set, 50-60 minutes.
   - The middle will be just a tad jiggly.
   - Cool completely, at least 1 hour, before removing from pan.

## Topping

1. Whisk milk (1 1/4 cups) and pudding mix (1 × 3/4oz package) together in a
   bowl until thoroughly combined.
1. Fold in whipped topping (1/2 cup).
1. Slice bananas and arrange on top of cooled cheesecake.
1. Spread pudding mixture on top.
1. Chill at least 3 hours before serving.
